1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small data files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used by online service providers to make their websites or services work, or to work more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information.

2. How We Use Cookies

We currently use cookies primarily for authentication and essential platform functionality. These strictly necessary cookies enable core features such as secure login, maintaining your session while you navigate Oríta, and remembering your preferences.

If we introduce additional analytics or advertising cookies in the future, this policy will be updated accordingly.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential/Strictly Necessary Cookies: These cookies are required for the operation of our platform. They include, for example, cookies that enable you to log into secure areas of Oríta.
  • Functional Cookies: These are used to recognize you when you return to our platform. This enables us to personalize our content for you and remember your preferences.

4. Managing Cookies

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can set or amend your web browser controls to accept or refuse cookies. If you choose to reject cookies, you may still use our platform though your access to some functionality and areas may be restricted.

Since the means by which you can refuse cookies through your web browser controls vary from browser to browser, you should visit your browser's help menu for more information.
