1. Authentic Experiences

We rely on genuine experiences to help others discover great places.

  • Honest Reviews: Only write reviews for businesses you have genuinely interacted with. Do not post fake, compensated, or retaliatory reviews.
  • Accurate Information: Do not create fake businesses or use sock-puppet accounts. Provide accurate location data and operating hours.

2. Respectful Interactions

Treat everyone on Oríta with respect.

  • No Hate Speech or Discrimination: We do not tolerate content that promotes violence, attacks, or demeans individuals or groups based on race, ethnicity, religion, disability, gender, age, or sexual orientation.
  • No Harassment: Do not engage in bullying, stalking, or harassment, either publicly in reviews or privately in direct messages.

3. Safe Discovery

Keep the platform safe for all users.

  • No Illegal Content or Services: Do not use Oríta to promote or sell illegal goods, counterfeit items, or illegal services.
  • No Scams or Fraud: Fraudulent listings and deceptive practices are strictly prohibited.
  • No Spam: Do not post repetitive content, irrelevant promotional material, or unsolicited marketing messages.

4. Protect the Community

Moderation on Oríta is a collaborative effort. We rely on you to help keep the platform clean. Please report:

  • Scams and fraudulent activity.
  • Impersonation of businesses or individuals.
  • Incorrect business information or intentionally misleading location data.
  • Abusive messages or reviews.

5. Enforcement

If we determine that content or an account violates these guidelines, we may remove the content, issue a warning, or suspend the account.
