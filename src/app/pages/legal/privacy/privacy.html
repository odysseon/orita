1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, improve, and protect Oríta. The types of data we collect include:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and a hashed version of your password.
  • Business Profile Data: If you create a business on Oríta, we collect the business name, photos, location, operating hours, and contact details.
  • User-Generated Content: This includes reviews, messages, saved lists, and any media you upload.
  • Location Data: We may collect location data when you search for places or businesses. We use mapping and geocoding services to help locate businesses and improve discovery.
  • Activity Data: We collect data about how you interact with the platform, such as your search history, follow relationships, and viewed tours.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, personalize, and improve the Oríta discovery experience.
  • Authenticate your account and secure your data.
  • Communicate with you about platform updates, security alerts, and administrative messages.
  • Analyze platform usage to improve features and performance.

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Oríta does not sell your personal information or use it for targeted third-party advertising. We may share your information in the following limited circumstances:

  • With Service Providers: We use cloud storage providers to securely store uploaded media and mapping services to provide location features. These providers only have access to the data necessary to perform their functions.
  • Public Content: Information you choose to make public, such as reviews or business profiles, is visible to others.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or legal process, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023.

4. Your Rights and Choices

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can manage your account settings or contact us to exercise these rights.

5. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational measures designed to protect your data. However, no security system is impenetrable, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we will notify you through the platform or by email.
